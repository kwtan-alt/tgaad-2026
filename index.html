Access and validation

  • Top Glove Tower entrance and exit boom gates remain open from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM; they close after 10:00 PM.
  • Staff entering before 5:30 PM must visit the Times Office for parking validation before 10:00 PM, or by 11:00 PM at the latest.
  • At the TG Open Site Car Park, boom gates remain closed due to pickleball court usage. Attendees may request assistance from the Times24 Office to validate a Touch 'n Go, debit or credit card for a parking fee waiver.

Eligibility conditions

  • Early dismissal is strictly for staff attending the Annual Dinner, to allow time for travel and preparation. It is not leave.
  • Confirmed attendees must inform their immediate superior of their early departure that day.
  • Not applicable to staff on annual, medical or other leave (full or partial day) on 29 October 2026. No replacement entitlement will be granted.
  • It is not additional overtime if staff work beyond normal hours on the event date.
  • It applies only to staff physically working at the listed locations.
